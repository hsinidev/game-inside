Game Inside

Game Overview

A puzzle game where you must navigate a grid of boxes and click on them to move the "outside" box.

  • Each time you click on a box, it will be moved one space in the chosen direction.
  • The goal is to get all boxes into the outermost row without falling off the grid.

How to Play

Controls: Click to move boxes, Arrow keys to move outside box.

Tips and Tricks for Success

  1. Start with small moves and build up your skills.
  2. Use the grid to your advantage - place boxes in a way that allows you to easily move them.
  3. Keep an eye on your progress and adjust your strategy as needed.
  4. Don't be afraid to try new things - it's often the smallest changes that make a big difference.

a Great Workout for Your Brain: The game requires problem-solving skills, reaction time, and strategic thinking – all of which are great exercise for your brain.
